01 / The pain

Bad claims don't stay in one document.

A supplier changes a specification. The ecommerce listing, sales quotation and support FAQ may still say something else. Search finds words; employees still have to reconstruct the relationships by hand.

01 / TIME

Manual detective work

Find the matching SKU, locate its exact revision, translate measurements, compare sources and hunt down every copy of the claim.

02 / RISK

False confidence

Two legitimate-looking sources disagree. A rushed person or an overconfident AI chooses one and tells the customer “yes.”

03 / COST

The error spreads

The same promise appears in the web shop and a quotation. Fixing only one document leaves the other wrong.

02 / The agent

From a question to a safe action.

A deliberately bounded, graph-driven decision workflow: automated investigation where the evidence is clear, human approval where it isn't.

1
Scan

Rank connected variants and surface those needing attention.

2
Trace

Follow exact variant, revision and source relationships in FalkorDB.

3
Compare

Show both outcomes when source specifications disagree.

4
Decide

Require a documented human choice before acting on conflict.

5
Prepare

Draft corrections for affected claims and record the decision.

03 / A real example — fictional data

Two documents. Two truths. One careful decision.

CATALOGUE
Maximum combined height: 35 cm.

For a mattress plus topper totaling 34 cm, this source implies a fit.

ENGINEERING DRAWING
Maximum combined height: 12 inches.

12 inches is exactly 30.48 cm. The same 34 cm combination would be unsupported.

PromiseGraph does not guess.

It shows both evidence paths and the documents that would be affected by each choice. After a reviewer selects the drawing, the graph identifies two unsupported customer promises, leaves a valid 28 cm FAQ alone, and does not touch a different bed variant. The decision and reasoning are saved.

Evidence, not marketing fiction. All example organisations, products and documents are synthetic. PromiseGraph verifies documented product claims, not physical fit.
